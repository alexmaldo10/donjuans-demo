comes alive.

Don Juan's Cantina is a modern take on the corner cantina you grew up dreaming about. Our kitchen leans into traditional flavors with creative twists, every plate handmade, every margarita poured with intention.

Pop in for a quick taco lunch, settle in for date night, or take over the patio with the whole crew. We've got a seat with your name on it.
